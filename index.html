VA hospitals earn record-high quality ratings in 2026 CMS report

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
The lead

Washington — The Department of Veterans Affairs announced that 78% of VA hospitals that received an Overall Hospital Quality Star Rating earned four or five stars in the latest annual report from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services.

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
In summary

That’s the highest percentage since CMS first started rating VA facilities in 2023. VA accounted for nearly 15% of all 5-star rated hospitals, and it’s the fourth consecutive year VA facilities have outperformed non-VA facilities.
